psp-measure's GE tests, round 10 (tools/psp-measure/ge.c in Phobos says what each test draws and runs;
docs/psp-core.md, part 112, what each is for). Records of words as round 8's (manifest8.txt): case, size, CPU
MHz << 16 | bus MHz (as scePower gave them back), repeats | values << 16, then per run `values` words: times in
microseconds from just before sceGeListEnQueue to the finish callback and to sceGeListEnQueue's return;
time-contention and time-contention2 two more, a thread's turns of its pattern while the GE drew and their time
(the GE idle: the first time is the thread's 10 ms); 0xffffffff for a run not finished (see <name>.stalled). All
run at 222/111 and 333/166 MHz but time-contention2, at 333/222/166; the clocks as found are put back after each.
