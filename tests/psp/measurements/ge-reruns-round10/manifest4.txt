psp-measure's GE tests, round 4 (tools/psp-measure/ge.c in Phobos says what each test draws): each .bin is the
target's pixels after one test, a little-endian 32-bit word each, row by row, 256x256; but lines-depth, the
depth buffer's 256x256 16-bit values (low half) read through VRAM's fourth copy; dxt1/3/5-colors 256x64 (the
texture's colors at the left, white blended by its alpha from x 128); dxt-layout 256x32.
lines-*: lines in through mode (lines-3d through the matrices), in 16x16 cells at every sixteenth of a pixel.
bbox: 256 cells, each white if the GE took its bounding box to be in sight (the cases are in ge.c).
curves-*: curved surfaces (BEZIER, SPLINE), mostly drawn as points, a vertex each: curves-bezier and -spline
their pixels and colors, -bezier-depths and -spline-depths the depth buffer's 256x256 values there (low half,
through VRAM's fourth copy); -places the vertices' sixteenths, -texels their texture coordinates, -made-up
texture coordinates a vertex type lacks, -lit normals made from the slopes, -culling, -joins which
vertices strips join, -count how many vertices at 16, 63 and 64 divisions (rows 0-2), -count-65, -100, -128,
-200 and -255 at that many (rows 3-7), one to a test.
stall-check: a list left at its stall address, so the GE doesn't finish it and is reset as after a stall;
16 divisions' count drawn before the reset (row 0) and after it (row 1).
<name>.stalled: the GE hadn't finished the test's list after 5 s and was reset: what the list held, how long it
waited, what the reset gave. Its .bin is what the GE had drawn.
curves-count.timed: the counts' runs left by the program before its GE waits were timed were cleared.
<name>.stopped: a test that stopped the PSP twice, given up on.
