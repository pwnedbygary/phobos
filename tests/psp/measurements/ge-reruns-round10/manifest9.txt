psp-measure's GE tests, round 9 (tools/psp-measure/ge.c in Phobos says what each test draws; docs/psp-core.md,
part 110, what each is for). Records of words as round 8's (manifest8.txt): case, size, CPU MHz << 16 | bus MHz
(as scePower gave them back), repeats | values << 16, then per run `values` times in microseconds from just
before sceGeListEnQueue: to the finish callback, to sceGeListEnQueue's return, and (time-lists2) to the last
sceGeListEnQueue's or sceGeListUpdateStallAddr's return; 0xffffffff for a run not finished (see
<name>.stalled). time-lists2 is round 8's time-lists with its stall addresses cached, at 222/111, 333/166,
333/222/166 and 266/133 MHz; the rest run at the first two; the clocks as found are put back after each.
